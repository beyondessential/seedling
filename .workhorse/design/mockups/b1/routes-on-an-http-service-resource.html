Routes on an http_service resource

What the spec says literally: http_service becomes a resource of its own, carrying the routes.

Declared in BSL

let web_svc = app.service("web").exported().http(80)
    .compress(#{ minimum_length: 1024 })
    .balance(#{ policy: "least_conn" });

web_svc.route("/api").balance(#{ try_duration: 10 });
web_svc.route("/v1").compress(false);
Identical BSL. Only the reporting surface differs.

Two rows named web. An http_service is a view of a service, not a thing that runs, so its row is always “No instances.” and its stop and scale controls have nothing to act on.
Views collide. Resources are keyed by kind and name, so a script calling both svc.http(80) and svc.http(3000) yields two views competing for one http_service row. Picking a winner is a new rule to specify, and neither port is more canonical than the other.
