Routes on the service def

Resolved compression and balancing hang off the existing service resource. No new resource kind.

Declared in BSL

let web_svc = app.service("web").exported().http(80)
    .compress(#{ minimum_length: 1024 })
    .balance(#{ policy: "least_conn" });

web_svc.route("/api").balance(#{ try_duration: 10 });
web_svc.route("/v1").compress(false);
Three routes come from the deployments' bindings (/ frontend, /api and /v1 api). Each field resolves on its own: /api keeps the service's least_conn and its own try duration.
